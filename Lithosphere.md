# Lithosphere (TNPSC Group II Geography)

## 1. Origin & Internal Structure of the Earth
* **Big Bang Theory**: The Universe was formed due to a cosmic explosion approximately 10 to 20 billion years ago.
* **Pangea & Panthalassa**: Originally, all continents were clustered around the South Pole in a supercontinent called **Pangea** (All Earth), surrounded by a mega-ocean called **Panthalassa** (All Ocean).
* **Lithospheric Plates**: Pangea broke into plates that move slowly (millimeters to centimeters per year).
* **Three Main Layers**:
  1. **Crust**: Uppermost layer. 
     * *Continental Crust*: **SIAL** (Silica + Aluminium).
     * *Oceanic Crust*: **SIMA** (Silica + Magnesium).
  2. **Mantle**: Lies between crust and core. Comprises **83%** of Earth's volume. 
     * *Upper Mantle*: Known as the **Asthenosphere** (extends up to 700 km).
     * *Lower Mantle*: Semi-solid and plastic. Average density = 8.
  3. **Core (Barysphere)**: Innermost layer, also known as **NIFE** (Nickel + Ferrous). Responsible for Earth's magnetic field. Density = 12.

## 2. Forces of Earth Movement
Changes on Earth's surface are brought by Endogenic (internal) and Exogenic (external) forces.
* **Endogenic Forces**: Originate within the crust.
  * **Slow Movements (Diastrophism)**:
    * *Epirogenic (Continental/Vertical)*: Leads to uplift or subsidence along faults. Creates **Block mountains**, plateaus, or **Rift valleys**.
    * *Orogenic (Mountain-building/Horizontal)*: Causes folding (Anticlines and Synclines). Responsible for **Fold Mountains**.
  * **Sudden Movements**: Destructive in nature, causing Earthquakes and Volcanic eruptions.

## 3. Earthquakes & Seismic Waves
* **Focus (Hypocenter)**: Point of origin inside the earth.
* **Epicenter**: Point on the surface directly above the focus.
* **Measurement Scales**:
  * **Richter Scale**: Measures *Magnitude* (energy released).
  * **Mercalli Scale** *(Source text refers to it as Marcauli Scale)*: Measures *Intensity* (visible damage).
* **Earthquake Waves**:
  * **Body Waves**: 
    * *P-Waves (Primary)*: Vibrate parallel to propagation. Speed: **8 km/s**. First to be recorded.
    * *S-Waves (Secondary)*: Vibrate perpendicular. Speed: **5 km/s**.
  * **Surface Waves (L-Waves)**: Last to be recorded. Speed: **4 km/s**. Cause wide-scale visible damage.
* **Causes**: Volcanicity, Folding/Faulting, Anthropogenic effects (dams, mining, nuclear tests), Plate tectonics.

## 4. Earthquake Zones in India
* **Himalayan Region**: Maximum intensity (subduction zone of Asiatic and Indian plates). Ex: Uttarkashi (1999).
* **Plain Region**: Moderate intensity. Ex: Bihar (1934), Assam (1956).
* **Peninsular India**: Minimum intensity.
* **Damage Risk Zones**: Divided into 5 zones. **Zone 5** (Very High Risk) includes J&K, Western Himachal, Uttarakhand, North-East India, and Kutch (Gujarat).

## 5. Plate Tectonics
* **Theory**: Proposed in 1967 by **McKenzie, Parker, and Morgan**. Lithosphere = Crust + Upper part of upper mantle.
* **Plates**: 7 Major plates (Antarctic, Pacific, North American, South American, India-Australia-New Zealand, African, Eurasian).
* **Plate Boundaries**:
  1. **Divergent**: Plates pull apart (spreading sites). Ex: **Mid-Atlantic Ridge**.
  2. **Convergent**: One plate dives under another (subduction zone). Causes high-magnitude earthquakes and explosive volcanoes.
  3. **Transform**: Plates slide horizontally past each other. Ex: Earthquakes in **California**.
* **Driving Force**: Convection cells driven by radioactive decay and residual heat. Molten material inside is **Magma**; on the surface, it is **Lava**.

## 6. Volcanoes
* **Active**: Erupts frequently. Ex: **Mauna Loa** (Hawaii - largest active), **Barren Island** (Only active in India).
* **Dormant (Sleeping)**: Erupted in the past, may erupt again. Ex: Vesuvius (Italy), Mauna Kea (Hawaii).
* **Extinct (Dead)**: No future eruptions expected. Ex: Mt. Kilimanjaro (Africa), Narcondam (Andaman), **Tiruvannamalai hills (TN)**, Panaka hills (AP).
* **Volcanic Gases**: **Water vapour (H2O)** is the most common (>60%), followed by CO2 (10-40%).

## 7. Tsunamis
* **Definition**: Seismic sea waves or "harbor waves" caused by high-magnitude undersea earthquakes, landslides, or volcanic eruptions.
* **Characteristics**: 
  * *Deep Water*: Very long wavelength, limited wave height (ships barely notice).
  * *Shallow Water*: Wavelength reduces, wave height increases drastically (up to 15m+), causing massive destruction.
* **Occurrence**: Frequent along the **Pacific Ring of Fire**.
* **Major Event**: 26th December 2004 tsunami (over 300,000 lives lost). India joined the International Tsunami Warning System post-2004.

## 8. Rocks (Petrology)
* **Petrology**: The science of rocks.
* **Igneous Rocks (Primary)**: Formed by cooling of magma/lava. Ex: Granite, Basalt, Gabbro.
* **Sedimentary Rocks**: Formed by lithification (compaction) of fragments. 
  * *Mechanical*: Sandstone, Conglomerate.
  * *Organic*: Chalk, Limestone, Coal.
  * *Chemical*: Gypsum, Halite.
* **Metamorphic Rocks**: Formed by Pressure, Volume, and Temperature (PVT) changes.
  * Granite → **Gneiss**
  * Basalt / Shale → **Schist**
  * Clay → **Slate**
  * Limestone → **Marble**
  * Sandstone → **Quartzite**
  * Coal → **Graphite**
