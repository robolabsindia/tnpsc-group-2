## 1. Introduction & Heart Anatomy
* **Essentials of Blood Circulation**: Blood, Heart (Pumping organ), and Vessels (Arteries, Veins, Capillaries).
* **Key Scientists**: 
  * **William Harvey**: Discovered that the heart acts as a pump.
  * **Fabricius** *(Text: Fabricious)*: First reported the presence of valves in veins.
* **Heart Structure**:
  * Hollow, muscular, fibrous organ; conical or pyramidal in shape.
  * Size of a clenched fist; Length: 12 cm, Diameter: 8-9 cm, Weight: 230-280 gms.
  * Contains **four chambers** and is situated in the **mediastinum** area.
  * Protected by a double-walled **pericardial membrane** (Outer: Fibrous, Middle: Parietal Cellular, Inner: Pericardium).
* **Heart Wall Layers**:
  1. **Epicardium**: Outer part.
  2. **Myocardium**: Middle part (made of muscle tissues, crucial for heart functioning).
  3. **Endocardium**: Inner part.

## 2. Double Circuit Circulation & Blood Vessels
Mammals possess a double circuit circulation system where blood flows through the heart twice:
1. **Pulmonary Circuit**: Right atrium → Right ventricle → Pulmonary artery → Lungs → Pulmonary vein → Left atrium.
2. **Hepatic Portal Circuit**: Body parts (spleen, pancreas, intestine, etc.) → Veins → Liver → Inferior vena cava → Right Atrium.

### Capillaries
* Made of **elastin and collagen fibers**; Size: 5-7 microns.
* Connect arteries to veins; transport O2, CO2, food, water, ions, vitamins, hormones, and antitoxins.
* **Vasa Vasorum**: The blood vessels that conduct blood circulation for the walls of larger blood vessels.

### Arteries vs. Veins
| Feature | Artery | Vein |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Function** | Distributing vessel | Collecting vessel |
| **Color** | Pink | Red |
| **Location** | Deep | Superficial |
| **Blood Type** | Pure/Oxygenated *(Exception: Pulmonary artery)* | Impure/Deoxygenated *(Exception: Pulmonary vein)* |
| **Pressure** | High pressure | Low pressure |
| **Wall** | Elastic, thick, more muscular | Non-elastic, collapsible, less muscular |
| **Valves** | Absent | Present (Internal valves) |
| **Branching** | Divides into arterioles (decreases in size) | Unites into venules (increases in size) |
| **Layers** | Tunica externa, media (thick), interna | Tunica externa, media (thin), interna |

## 3. Heart Valves & Blood Flow Pathway
### Valves
1. **Bicuspid / Mitral Valve**: Left auriculo-ventricular opening.
2. **Tricuspid Valve**: Right auriculo-ventricular opening.
3. **Semilunar Valve**: Opening of the aorta from the ventricle.
4. **Haversian Valve**: End of the opening of the superior vena cava.
5. **Eustachian Valve** *(Text: Eustactian)*: End of the opening of the inferior vena cava.
6. **Thebesian Valve**: Opening of the coronary sinus.

### Blood Flow Pathway
1. Impure blood from the body is collected by the **inferior and superior vena cava** and poured into the **right atrium** via the **coronary sinus** (which collects impure blood from heart walls).
2. Pours into the **right ventricle** through the **tricuspid valve**.
3. Departs through the **pulmonary artery** to the lungs.
4. After purification, returns via **pulmonary veins** to the **left atrium**.
5. Passes through the **bicuspid valve** to the **left ventricle**.
6. Pumped out through the **aorta** to supply all body parts.

## 4. Heart Induction & Cardiac Cycle
* **Conduction System**:
  * **SA Node** (Sino-auricular node / Pacemaker / Keith & Flack Node): Located in the upper corner of the right atrium (1.5 cm x 3 mm).
  * **AV Node**: Located in the lower left corner of the right atrium near the septum.
  * Impulse passes from AV node → **Bundle of His** → **Purkinje fibers**.
* **Heart Beat Rates**:
  * Adult Man: **72 per minute**
  * Children: **100 per minute**
  * Newborn Baby: **120-140 per minute**
* **Cardiac Cycle (0.8 sec total)**:
  * *Auricular*: Systole (0.1 min) + Diastole (0.7 min) = 0.8 min
  * *Ventricular*: Systole (0.3 min) + Diastole (0.5 min) = 0.8 min
* **Abnormalities**: **Bradycardia** (less than normal), **Tachycardia** (more than normal).

## 5. Heart Sounds, Blood Pressure & ECG
* **Heart Sounds** *(Heard via Stethoscope invented by **Rene Laennec**)*:
  * **First Sound (LUBB)**: Prolonged, dull sound (0.16-0.9 sec). Created by the closure of atrio-ventricular valves during ventricular systole.
  * **Second Sound (DUBB)**: Short, high-pitch sound (0.10 sec). Created by the closure of semilunar valves during ventricular diastole.
* **Blood Pressure**:
  * Measured by **Sphygmomanometer** (Invented by **Korotkoff** in 1905) at the brachial *(Text: branchial)* artery.
  * Normal BP: **120 (Systolic) / 80 (Diastolic) mm Hg**.
* **Electrocardiogram (ECG)**:
  * Invented by **Einthoven (1906)**; First recorded by **Waller (1907)**.
  * **P wave**: Activation of SA node (0.20 sec).
  * **PQ wave**: Interval of atrial contraction (P-R interval = 0.25 sec).
  * **RS wave**: Speed of impulse from AV node to Bundle of His/Purkinje (QRS interval = 0.35 sec).
  * **ST wave**: Interval period of ventricular ejection (T wave = 0.20 sec).
