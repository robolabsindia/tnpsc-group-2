## 1. Agricultural Land Use & Cropping Intensity
* **Land Use Distribution**:
  * **Net Sown Area**: 46%
  * **Forest**: 23%
  * **Area not available for cultivation**: 14%
  * **Fallow Land** (out of cultivation for 1 to 5 years): 8%
  * **Agricultural Land** (Net sown area + Fallow land): 54%
  * **Waste Land**: 5%
  * **Pastures and Tree Crops**: 5%
* **Intensity of Cropping**:
  * **Formula**: `(Total cropped area / Net sown area) * 100`
  * Refers to the number of crops raised on a field during an agricultural year.
  * **Top States**: Punjab (189%), Uttar Pradesh (180%), Himachal Pradesh (170%), Haryana (168%), West Bengal (166%), Mizoram (100%).

## 2. Crop Seasons
* **Kharif (June - September)**: Rice, Jute, Maize, Cotton, Bajra, Jowar, Ragi, Groundnut.
* **Rabi (October - March)**: Wheat, Gram, Rapeseeds, Mustard, Barley, Jowar.
* **Zaid (April - June)**: Fruits, Vegetables, and fodder crops.
```
```
## 3. Major Food Crops
### Rice
* **Status**: India is the 2nd largest producer and consumer. Occupies 1/4th of the net sown area in the eastern and western parts of the country.
* **Requirements**: High heat and humidity. Temp: 20-30°C. Rainfall: 150 cm. Soil: Clayey.
* **Top Producers**: West Bengal, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh.

### Wheat
* **Status**: Sown in early winter, harvested in early summer. India is the 4th largest producer globally.
* **Requirements**: Mid-latitude grasslands. Temp: 10-15°C. Rainfall: 100 cm. Soil: Loamy and clayey.
* **Top Producers**: Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana.

### Maize
* **Uses**: Food, fodder, and manure.
* **Requirements**: Temp: 21-27°C. Rainfall: 50-100 cm. Soil: Alluvial / Red soil.
* **Top Producers**: Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra.

### Millets
* **Jowar**: Temp 16-30°C, Rainfall 30-100 cm. (Maharashtra, Karnataka)
* **Bajra**: Dry and warm climate. Temp 25-30°C, Rainfall 40-50 cm. (Maharashtra, Gujarat)
* **Ragi (Finger Millet)**: Temp 20-30°C, Rainfall 50-100 cm. (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Uttarakhand, Maharashtra)
* **Barley**: Cannot tolerate high heat and humidity. Temp 10-15°C, Rainfall 75-100 cm. (Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan)

## 4. Cash Crops
* **Cotton**: Temp 21-30°C, Rainfall 50-100 cm. Requires at least 210 frost-free days. (Gujarat, Maharashtra, AP)
* **Jute**: Temp 24-35°C, Rainfall 120-150 cm. (West Bengal, Bihar, Assam)
* **Sugarcane**: Maturing period 10-15 months. India is the 2nd largest producer. Temp 21-27°C, Rainfall 75-150 cm. (UP, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu - noted for long crushing period and high sucrose content).
* **Tobacco**: Temp 16-35°C, Rainfall 100 cm. (Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat)
* **Tea**: Tropical/subtropical, hot and humid. Temp 20-30°C, Rainfall 150-300 cm, Altitude 600-1800m. (Assam, WB, Kerala, TN)
* **Coffee**: Hot and humid. Temp 15-28°C, Rainfall 150-250 cm, Altitude 600-1600m. (Karnataka, Kerala, TN)
* **Rubber**: Hot and humid. Temp 25-35°C, Rainfall >200 cm, Altitude 300-450m (Not above 700m). (Kerala, TN, Karnataka, Tripura)

## 5. Livestock Census & Distribution
* **Census History**: Started in 1919. The 2012 census was the 19th in the series.
* **Total Population (2012)**: 512.05 million (decreased by 3.33% over the previous census). Includes cattle, buffalo, sheep, goat, pig, horses, mules, donkeys, camels, mithun, and yak.
* **Distribution (%)**: Cattle (37.28%), Goat (26.40%), Buffalo (21.23%), Sheep (12.71%), Pigs (2.01%), Others (0.37%).
* **Largest Population by State**:
  * **Buffalo**: Uttar Pradesh
  * **Cattle**: Madhya Pradesh
  * **Sheep**: Andhra Pradesh
  * **Goat**: Rajasthan
  * **Pig**: Assam
  * **Camel**: Rajasthan
  * **Mithun**: Arunachal Pradesh
  * **Horses & Mules**: Uttar Pradesh
  * **Donkeys**: Rajasthan

## 6. Fisheries & Forest Cover
* **Fisheries**:
  * Engages 14 million people. Provides nutritional security and contributes to agricultural exports.
  * **Total Production**: 6.4 mmt (Inland: 3.4 mmt / 55%; Marine: 3.0 mmt).
  * **Top Fish Producing States**: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu.
* **India State of Forest Report**:
  * Total Geographical Area: 32,87,263 sq.km.
  * **Forest Cover**: 21.06% (6,92,394 sq.km).
  * **Top States by Forest Cover**: 1. Madhya Pradesh (Maximum area), 2. Arunachal Pradesh, 3. Chhattisgarh.
