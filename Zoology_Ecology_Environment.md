## 1. Introduction to Ecology & Ecosystem
* **Ecology**: Derived from a Greek word meaning "house" or "living area". 
  * *Introduced by*: Reiter.
  * *Father of Ecology*: Ernst Haeckel (1869).
  * *Father of Indian Ecology*: R. Misra.
* **Ecosystem**: The complex of a community of organisms and its environment functioning as an ecological unit. 
  * *Introduced by*: A.G. Tansley (1935).
* **Branches of Ecology**: Autecology (individual species), Synecology (natural communities), Gynecology (gene frequency), Paleoecology (ancient organisms), Applied, Systems, and Bioecology.
* **Types of Ecosystems**:
  * *Forest*: Tropical rainforest and deciduous (23% of India's land).
  * *Grassland*: Steppes (shorter grass) and Savannas (taller grass).
  * *Desert*: Hot (Thar) and Cold (Ladakh/Tibet). Rainfall < 25 cm.
  * *Aquatic*: Marine (largest, most stable) and Freshwater (Lentic/standing like ponds; Lotic/running like rivers).
  * *Coastal*: Saline, brackish, and coastlines.
* **Components**: 
  * *Biotic*: Producers (autotrophs), Consumers (herbivores, carnivores, omnivores), Decomposers (bacteria, fungi).
  * *Abiotic*: Light, temperature, water, soil, etc.

## 2. Food Chains, Webs & Ecological Pyramids
* **Food Chain**: Sequence of who eats whom. Trophic levels range from primary producers to top predators.
* **Food Web**: A network of interconnected and interdependent food chains.
* **Ecological Pyramids**:
  * *Pyramid of Numbers & Biomass*: Can be upright (terrestrial) or inverted (ponds).
  * *Pyramid of Energy*: **Always upright**.
* **10% Law**: Given by **Lindeman (1942)**. Only 10% of energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next.

## 3. Biogeochemical Cycles
* **Nitrogen Cycle**: Nitrogen fixation (N2 to NH4+), Nitrification (ammonia to nitrite/nitrate), Plant metabolism, Ammonification, and Denitrification (NO3- back to N2). Disruption causes tropospheric smog and greenhouse gases (nitrous oxide).
* **Carbon Cycle**: CO2 is fixed by plants via photosynthesis and returned via respiration, decomposition, and burning of fossil fuels. Acts as a greenhouse gas.
* **Oxygen Cycle**: Driven mainly by photosynthesis (phytoplankton, Prochlorococcus) and photolysis. Lost via respiration, decay, and chemical weathering (e.g., rust formation).

## 4. Pollution & Waste Management
* **Land Pollution**: Caused by urbanization, industrialization, and improper waste disposal. 
  * *Bioplastics*: Made by microorganisms (*Alcaligenes*); homopolymer type called **polyhydroxybutyrate**.
* **Solid Waste Management**: 
  * *Classification*: Biodegradable, Non-biodegradable, Toxic, Non-toxic, Biomedical.
  * *Disposal Methods*: Segregation, Dumping, Composting, Incineration (best for pharmaceutical waste), Scrubbers (removes dry fly ash/SO2), and Electrostatic Precipitators (99% effective for smoke/particulates).
  * *3 R's*: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle.
* **Water Pollution**: 
  * *Effects*: Eutrophication, Algal blooms, O2 deficiency.
  * *Control*: *Nitrosomonas europaea* (bacteria to demolish microbes in domestic waste), Eucalyptus trees (absorb minerals).
* **Air Pollution**:
  * *Acid Rain*: Caused by SO2 and NO2 combining with water (H2SO4, HNO3). Affects Taj Mahal, soil, and water.
  * *Ozone Depletion*: Caused by CFCs/ODS. Ozone hole discovered in **1984 (NASA)** over **Antarctica** (coined by **Shaan Bean**). Measured in **Dobson Units (DU)**. Montreal Protocol bans CFCs.
  * *Greenhouse Effect*: CO2 (60%), CH4 (20%), N2O (16%), CFCs (6%).
* **Sound Pollution**: Measured in decibels (dB). Jet planes (145 dB), Traffic (90 dB), Vacuum cleaner (85 dB), Speech (60 dB).
* **Radioactive Pollution**: Causes DNA damage, free radicals, and cancer.

## 5. Environmental Movements & Global Initiatives
* **Kyoto Protocol (1997)**: International treaty setting binding obligations on industrialized countries to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Global Village**: Term coined by **Marshall McLuhan**. (Global Village Tech Park is located in Bangalore).
* **M.S. Swaminathan Research Foundation (MSSRF)**: Established in 1988 for pro-nature, pro-poor, pro-women sustainable livelihoods.
* **Bishnois Movement**: Khejarli village (Rajasthan); sacrificed lives to protect trees by hugging them.
* **Chipko Movement**: Led by **Sunderlal Bahuguna**; act of hugging trees to stop open felling in Garhwal/Vindhyas/Western Ghats.
* **Silent Valley Movement (1973)**: Kerala; saved the evergreen tropical forest and **Kunthipuzha river** from a hydroelectric project. Declared a National Park in 1985.
* **Narmada Bachao Andolan (NBA)**: Led by **Medha Patkar and Baba Amte** against large dams (like Sardar Sarovar) on the Narmada river.
* **Blue Cross Movement**: Motive is animal welfare.
