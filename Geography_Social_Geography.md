## 1. Population & Density (Census 2011)
* **Total Population**: 121.02 crore (1.21 billion). India is the 2nd most populous country after China.
* **Global Share**: Represents **17.31%** of the total world population.
* **Decadal Growth (2001-2011)**: 17.64%.
* **Gender Split**: 623.7 million males and 586.5 million females.
* **Average Density**: 382 persons per sq.km.
* **Highest Density**: 
  * *Union Territory*: NCT of Delhi (11,297 persons/sq.km).
  * *State*: Bihar (1,102 persons/sq.km).
* **Lowest Density**: Arunachal Pradesh (17 persons/sq.km).
* *Note*: Nagaland witnessed a decline in density (from 120 to 119) due to a negative population growth rate.
```
```
## 2. Sex Ratio & Literacy
* **Sex Ratio**: 940 females per 1000 males (improved from 933 in 2001).
  * **Highest (State)**: Kerala (1084) - *The only state with more females than males*.
  * **Highest (UT)**: Puducherry (1038).
  * **Lowest (State)**: Haryana (877).
  * **Tamil Nadu**: 995 (most even sex ratio among major states).
* **Literacy Rate**: 74.04% (Male: 82.14%, Female: 65.91%).
  * **Highest Female Literacy**: Kerala (93.91%).
  * **Lowest Female Literacy**: Bihar (53.33%).
  * **Highest UT Literacy**: Lakshadweep (92.28%).
  * **Lowest UT Literacy**: Dadra & Nagar Haveli (77.65%).
```
```
## 3. Decadal Growth Trends
* **Historical Trend**: Population has continuously grown since 1921. The decade **1911-21** was the only period of negative growth.
* **Highest Growth (UTs)**: Dadra & Nagar Haveli (55.50%), Daman & Diu (53.54%).
* **Highest Growth (States)**: Meghalaya (27.82%), Andhra Pradesh (25.92%), Bihar (25.07%), Chhattisgarh (25.59%).
* **Lowest Growth (Large State)**: Kerala (4.86%).
* **Negative Growth**: Nagaland (-0.47%).

## 4. Important Tribal Groups of India
* **Abhors**: Mongoloid stock in North-East India.
* **Badagas, Irula, Kanikar, Kotas, Todas, Yurva**: Native tribes of **Tamil Nadu** (Todas and Kotas are specific to the Nilgiri Hills).
* **Bakkarwals & Gujjars**: Animal/sheep rearers of J&K and Himachal Pradesh.
* **Bhils**: Dravidian stock, now in Central India (MP, Rajasthan).
* **Gonds**: Inhabit forests in MP, Jharkhand, AP, and Odisha.
* **Jarawas, Onges, Sentinelese, Shompens**: Indigenous tribes of the **Andaman & Nicobar Islands**.
* **Lepchas**: Original inhabitants of Sikkim.
* **Moplahs**: Muslims of the Malabar district in Kerala.
* **Santhals**: West Bengal, Jharkhand, and Odisha.
* **Varlis**: Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Dadra & Nagar Haveli.
