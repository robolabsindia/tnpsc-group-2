## 1. Introduction & Classification
* **Definition**: A mineral is a natural substance of organic or inorganic origin with definite chemical and physical properties. A rock with a large concentration of a specific mineral is called its **ore**.
* **Historical Ages**: Stone Age → Copper Age → Bronze Age (Copper + Tin) → Iron Age (~3000 years ago) → Steel Age (Modern).
* **Classification**:
  * **Metallic Minerals**: 
    * *Ferrous* (contain iron): Iron, manganese, nickel, cobalt, tungsten.
    * *Non-ferrous* (no iron): Gold, silver, copper, bauxite.
  * **Non-Metallic Minerals**: Mica, limestone, gypsum, potash.
  * **Mineral Fuels**: Coal and petroleum.
* **Distribution Belts**: Northeastern Plateau, Southwestern Plateau, and Northwestern region.
```
```
## 2. Iron Ore
* **Significance**: Backbone of modern civilization. India has **20%** of the world's total reserves. 75% of Indian reserves are of Haematite grade.
* **Types of Iron Ore**:
  * **Haematite**: Reddish (red-ochre), **60-70%** iron content.
  * **Magnetite**: Dark brown/black (black ore), magnetic, **70%** iron content.
  * **Limonite**: Yellow/light brown (hydrated iron oxide), **40-60%** iron content.
  * **Siderite**: Inferior variety (iron carbonate), **20-40%** iron content.
* **Top Reserve States**: Jharkhand (25.7%), Orissa (21.6%), Karnataka (20.7%), MP-Chattisgarh (18.5%), Goa (10.5%).
* **Production**: India is the largest producer in Asia and 7th in the world.
* **Tamil Nadu**: Found in Salem, North Arcot, Tiruchchirappalli, Coimbatore, and Madurai (35-50% iron content).
```
```
## 3. Manganese
* **Uses**: Making iron/steel, ferro-manganese alloys, bleaching powder, dry cell batteries.
* **Global Rank**: India is the **5th largest producer** (after Brazil, Gabon, South Africa, Australia).
* **Top Producers**: 
  * **Orissa**: Largest producer (33%). Districts: Keonjhar, Sundargarh.
  * **Madhya Pradesh**: 3rd largest (20%). Districts: Balaghat, Chhindwara.
  * **Karnataka**: Largest *reserves*, but 4th largest producer (14%). District: Bellary.
```
```
## 4. Bauxite & Limestone
* **Bauxite**: Clay-like ore of **aluminium**. Requires cheap electricity to convert to aluminium.
  * **Global Rank**: India has the **5th largest reserves** (after Guinea, Australia, Brazil, Vietnam).
  * **Top States**: Orissa and Andhra Pradesh hold >50% of reserves.
* **Limestone**: Calcium carbonate. Sedimentary origin.
  * **Uses**: **75%** in cement industry, 16% in iron & steel.
  * **Top Producers**: MP, Chattisgarh, AP, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Karnataka.
```
```
## 5. Coal (Mineral Fuel)
* **Origin**: Organic substance (hydrocarbons) in sedimentary rocks formed from buried forests.
* **Types of Coal**:
  * **Peat**: <40% carbon. High moisture, large ash.
  * **Lignite**: 40-60% carbon. Soft, compact. Found in Rajasthan, TN, Assam, J&K.
  * **Bituminous**: 60-80% carbon. "Bunker coal". Used for coke (iron/steel smelting). Found in Jharkhand, Orissa, WB, MP, Chhattisgarh.
  * **Anthracite**: 80-90% carbon. Highest heating capacity, smokeless. Found only in J&K.
* **Coalfields**: 
  * *Gondwana*: River valleys (Damodar, Mahanadi, Son, Godavari).
  * *Tertiary*: Assam, Meghalaya, TN, Rajasthan.
* **Production**: India is the **4th largest** coal producer. 80% is bituminous (non-cooking). **75% of lignite** comes from **Neyveli (Tamil Nadu)**.
* **Mining Problems**: Uneven distribution, non-coking grade, transport bottlenecks, pilferage, and mine fires.
```
```
## 6. Petroleum & Refineries
* **Origin**: Organic decay in marine sedimentary basins.
* **Producing Regions**:
  * **Brahmaputra Valley**: Oldest region (Digboi, Moran-Hugrijan, NaharKatiya).
  * **Gujarat Coast**: Gulf of Cambay (Ankleshwar, Kalol).
  * **Off-Shore**: **Mumbai High** (discovered 1974 by ONGC), Bassein, Aliabet, and East Coast deltas (Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri).
* **Oil Refineries (Chronological Highlights)**:
  * **Digboi (Assam)**: 1901 (First refinery in India).
  * **Trombay (MH)**: 1954/1955.
  * **Vishakhapatnam (AP)**: 1957.
  * **Noonmati (Assam)**: 1962.
  * **Barauni (Bihar)**: 1964.
  * **Koyali (Gujarat)**: 1965.
  * **Kochi (Kerala)**: 1966.
  * **Chennai (TN)**: 1969.
  * **Haldia (WB)**: 1975.
  * **Mathura (UP)**: 1982.
  * **Mangalore (KA)**: 1996.
  * **Panipat (Haryana)**: 1997.
  * **Jamnagar (Gujarat)**: 1999 (RIL).
