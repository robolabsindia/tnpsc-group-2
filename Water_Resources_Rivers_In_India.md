# Water Resources & Rivers in India (TNPSC Group II Geography)

## 1. India: Location, Size & Boundaries
* **Location**: 8°4’N to 37°6’N Latitudes; 68°7’E to 97°25’E Longitudes. The Tropic of Cancer (23½°) passes through the middle.
* **Coastline**: Mainland = 6,100 km; Total (with islands) = **7,516 km**.
* **Area**: 32,87,263 sq.km (2.4% of world land). 7th largest country globally.
* **Important Boundaries**:
  * **Durand Line**: India/Pakistan with Afghanistan.
  * **McMahon Line**: India-China Eastern boundary (1140 km, 1913-14 Shimla convention).
  * **Radcliff Line**: India-Pakistan (1947 partition).
* **Key Agreements**: Panchsheel (1954, India-China), Tashkant (1966, India-Pak), Shimla (1972, India-Pak).

## 2. Physiography of India
* **Relief Distribution**: Mountains (10.7%), Hilly (18.6%), Plateau (27.7%), Plains (43%).
* **The Himalayas**:
  * **Great Himalayas (Himadri)**: Northernmost. Contains Mt. Everest (8850m - Sagarmatha/Chomlungma).
  * **Passes**: Burzil, Zojila *[Source Note: Text states Jarkhand, geographically J&K]*, Bara Lachala, Shipkila (HP), Thaga La, Niti, Lipul Lekh (UK), Nathu La (Sikkim).
  * **Lesser Himalayas (Himachal)**: Pirpanjal, Dhaula Dhar, Mussoorie, Nag Tiba.
  * **Shiwalik (Outer)**: Jammu Hills, Dafla, Misi, Abor, Mishimi. Longitudinal valleys are **'Duns'** (west) and **'Duars'** (east). Ex: Dehra Dun.
  * **Trans-Himalayas**: Zaskar, Ladakh, Kailas, Karakoram. **K2 (Godwin Austin)** is the 2nd highest peak in the world and highest in the Indian Union. Ladakh is a cold desert.
* **North Indian Plain (Gangetic)**: 6,52,000 sq.km. Alluvial and fertile. Known as the **"Heart of India"** (houses 40% of the population).
* **Peninsular Plateau**: Oldest landmass. Divided by the **Narmada Rift Valley** into Malwa and Deccan plateaus.
  * **Malwa**: Bounded by Aravalli & Vindhya. Highest peak: **Guru Shikhar**.
  * **Deccan**: Bounded by Satpura, Western & Eastern Ghats. **Anaimudi (2,695m)** in the Annamalai range is the highest peak in South India. Western & Eastern Ghats meet at the **Nilgiri hills**.
* **Coastal Plains**:
  * **Western**: 1,500 km long. Ports: Mumbai, Marmagoa, Cochin, Mangalore, Kandla.
  * **Eastern**: Broad and flat with deltas (Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri). Contains Chilka (Odisha) and Pulicat (TN) lakes. Divided into Coromandel (South) and Utkal (North).
* **Islands**: 247 total (204 in BoB, 43 in Arabian Sea).
  * **Andaman & Nicobar**: Volcanic. **Indira Point** (formerly Pigmalion Point) is the southernmost tip, submerged in the 2004 Tsunami.
  * **Lakshadweep**: Coral origin.
  * **Majuli**: Largest river island in the world *[Source Note: Text states Manipur, geographically Assam]*.

## 3. Water Resources & Drainage Patterns
* **Global Water**: 96.5% in oceans, 2.5% fresh water (76% locked in ice/glaciers). India receives 4% of global precipitation and ranks **133rd** in per capita water availability.
* **Drainage Patterns**:
  * **Antecedent**: Rivers that existed before the Himalayas (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra).
  * **Consequent**: Follow the general slope (Most Peninsular rivers).
  * **Subsequent**: Erode weak rock layers and meet main rivers at right angles (Chambal, Sind, Ken, Betwa, Son).
  * **Dendritic**: Tree-like branching (Indo-Gangetic plains).

## 4. Major River Systems
### Himalayan Rivers
* **Indus**: Originates at Bokhar Chu (Mansarovar). Length: 2880 km (709 km in India).
  * *Tributaries*: Jhelum (Srinagar), Chenab (Largest), Ravi, Beas (Entirely in India), Satluj (Only one not originating in India).
* **Ganga**: Originates at Gangotri (Bhagirathi + Alaknanda meet at Devprayag).
  * *Key Tributaries*: Yamuna, Chambal (Ravines), Son (Amarkantak), Damodar (Rift valley). Forms the **Sunderbans** delta.
* **Brahmaputra**: Originates at Chemayungdung (Tibet). Makes a U-turn at Namcha Barwa. Known as Dihang in AP, Jamuna/Meghna in Bangladesh.

### Peninsular Rivers
* **Narmada**: Originates at Amarkantak. Flows through a rift valley. Features **Dhuandhar Fall** and Marble Rocks. **Sardar Sarovar Dam** is built across it.
* **Godavari**: Originates at Trimbuk (Nashik). Longest in South India (**Dakshin Ganga**).
* **Krishna**: Originates at Mahabaleshwar. Tributaries include Tungbhadra and Bhima.
* **Kaveri**: Originates at Brahmagiri. Forms **Sivasamudram** (2nd biggest waterfall in India).
* **Others**:
  * **Luni**: Originates in Pushkar, lost in the Rann of Kutch.
  * **Shravati**: Forms **Jog/Gersoppe Falls** (Highest in India).
  * **Bharathpuza**: Longest river in Kerala.

## 5. Geography of Tamil Nadu
* **Size**: 11th largest state (1,30,058 sq.km).
* **Rainfall**: Normal annual rainfall is **945 mm**. **48%** from NE Monsoon, **32%** from SW Monsoon.
* **Zones**: 7 aggro-climatic zones (Cauvery Delta is the most fertile).
* **Coastline**: **1,076 km** (2nd longest in India after Gujarat). Forms the Coromandel Coast. 13 coastal districts. Pamban Island separates the Gulf of Mannar and Palk Strait.
