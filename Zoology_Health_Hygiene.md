## 2. Caloric Requirements & BMI
* **Daily Caloric Requirements (kcal)**:

* **Body Mass Index (BMI)**:
  * **Formula**: Body weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²
  * **18.5 to 24.9**: Correct / Normal weight
  * **Below 18**: Low weight (Underweight)
  * **25 to 30**: Excess weight (Overweight)

## 4. The Immune System
### Innate / Natural Immunity (Non-Specific)
* **Physical Barriers**: Skin
* **Physiological Barriers**: Acid in the stomach, Saliva in the mouth
* **Cellular Barriers**: White Blood Cells (WBCs)
* **Cytokine Barriers**: Viral-infected cells produce **interferons**.

### Adaptive / Acquired Immunity (Specific)
* **Active Immunity**:
  * *Natural*: Recovery from an actual disease.
  * *Artificial*: Immunization / Vaccination.
* **Passive Immunity**:
  * *Natural*: Mother to child via placenta; Mother's milk (**Colostrum** contains abundant **IgA** antibodies).
  * *Artificial*: Injection of immune serum (e.g., **Gamma globulin**).
