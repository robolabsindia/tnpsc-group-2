## 1. Reproduction – Definition & Types
* **Reproduction**: A process by which one organism produces its offspring (phylogeny).
* **Two Modes**: Asexual Reproduction & Sexual Reproduction.

### Asexual Reproduction
* Takes place in **body cells** through **mitosis**.
* Done by a **single parent**.

| Mode | Organism |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Binary Fission** | Bacteria, Amoeba, Flatworm |
| **Multiple Fission** | Amoeba |
| **Budding** | Hydra, Yeast, Sponge, Coelenterates |
| **Gemmules** | Sea Sponges |
| **Regeneration** | Echinoderms |
| **Syngamy** | Cockroach, Frog, Human being |

### Sexual Reproduction
* Takes place in **reproductive cells**.
* **Meiosis** occurs first, followed by **fertilization**, then **mitosis**.
* An important process of nature; produces **healthier new generations**.

### Types of Sexual Processes
| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Conjugation** | Hereditary exchange |
| **Cytogamy** | Cytoplasmic fusion of two cells |
| **Karyogamy** | Nucleus fusion of two cells |
| **Automixis** | Fusion of germ cells |
| **Syngamy** | Fusion of male and female cells |

### Sub-types of Syngamy
* **Autogamy**, **Exogamy**, **Hologamy**, **Pedogamy**, **Mesogamy**, **Isogamy**, **Anisogamy**, **Macrogamy**, **Microgamy**

## 2. Viviparous vs. Oviparous
| Feature | Viviparous | Oviparous |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Organisms** | Most Mammals | Reptiles, Birds |
| **Development** | Embryo develops **inside** female body | Fertilized egg covered with calcium layer, kept **outside** body |
| **Birth** | New one comes out directly | Hatched until new one comes out of egg |

## 6. Male Reproductive Organs

### Vas Deferens (Ductus Deferens)
* Emerges from the **tail end of epididymis**.
* Ascends along the **posterior side of testis**.
* Associated with blood vessels and nerves → forms **Spermatic Cord**.
* **Spermatic Cord** consists of: Vas deferens, testicular artery, venous plexus, lymph vessels, nerves, fibrous processes and muscles.
* End enlarges to form **Ampulla**.
* Surrounded by smooth muscles → **peristaltic contraction** → propels sperm.

### Ejaculatory Duct
* Nearer to ampulla → **seminal vesicles** (sac-like).
* Joins ductus deferens → **Ejaculatory duct**.
* About **2.5 cm long**.
* Projects into **prostate gland**, opens into **urethra**.

### Urethra
* Extends from **urinary bladder** to distal end of **penis**.
* About **20 cm long**.
* Passage for **both urine and reproductive fluids**.
* **Three Parts**:
  1. **Prostatic Urethra**: Closest to bladder, passes through prostate gland.
  2. **Membranous Urethra**: Shortest part.
  3. **Spongy (Penile) Urethra**: Longest part; through the penis; has **urethral glands** (mucus-secreting).

### Penis
* **Male copulatory organ**.
* Two parts: **Radix (root)** & **Corpus (body)**.
* Radix attaches to **lower abdomen**.
* Corpus is **pendulous**, covered by **loose skin**.
* Three masses of **erectile tissue**:
  * Right & Left **Corpora Cavernosa** (majority)
  * Median **Corpus Spongiosum Penis** (surrounds urethra)
* Corpus spongiosum expands near end → **Glans Penis**.
* Swollen base = **Corona Glandis**.
* Skin folded at tip → **Prepuce (Foreskin)**.
* **Preputial glands** on corona glandis and penile neck.

### Seminal Vesicles
* **Two sac-like structures** between bladder and rectum.
* Each about **5 cm long**.
* Secretions contribute about **70%** of seminal fluid.

### Prostate Gland
* **Firm structure**, partly glandular, partly fibromuscular.
* Found around **beginning of male urethra**.
* About **3 cm in diameter**, weighs about **8 g**.
* Secretes **3-5 ml** seminal fluid.
* After middle age: often **enlarges**, may project into bladder, interrupt urination.

### Bulbo-urethral Gland
* **Two glands**, small round masses, about **1 cm in diameter**.
* Lie **lateral to membranous urethra**.
* Secretion may control **genito-urinary diseases**.

### Scrotum
* **Fibromuscular sac** containing testes and associated ducts.
* Divided into right and left by **cutaneous raphe**.
* **Left side is usually lower**.
* External appearance varies with **age and body temperature**.
* Scrotal skin: thin, pigmented, numerous **sweat glands** and **nerve endings**.

## 7. Female Reproductive Organs

### Ovaries
* **Paired structures**, one on each side of uterus in **pelvic region**.
* **Greyish pink** in colour.
* **Almond shaped**.
* Size: ~**3 cm long, 1.5 cm wide, 1 cm thick**.
* Attached by **mesovarium** (membranous fold).
* Supported by **suspensory** and **ovarian ligaments**.

#### Ovarian Structure
* Surface covered by **ovarian surface epithelium** (single layer of cuboidal cells).
* Beneath epithelium: **tunica albuginea** (collagenous tissue).
* Two regions: **Cortex** (contains ovarian follicles) & **Medulla** (blood vessels, nerves at hilum).
* After puberty: **cortex** forms major part.
* Contains **ovarian follicles** and **corpora lutea** of various sizes.
* Stroma composed of **collagen**; follicles embedded in stroma.

### Ovarian Follicles (Development Stages)
* At birth: **primordial follicles** with **primary oocytes** (~25 µm), surrounded by single layer of flat follicular cells.

#### 1. Primary Follicle
* Follicle cells: squamous → **cuboidal**.
* **Membrana granulosa** becomes multilayered.
* Oocyte increases in size; outer thick layer = **zona pellucida**.
* Follicular cells divide → **granulosa cells**.

#### 2. Secondary Follicle
* About **20 µm thick**.
* Granulosa cells surround oocyte → **cumulus oophorus**.
* **Theca interna** & **theca externa** become prominent.

#### 3. Tertiary (Graafian) Follicle
* Only **one follicle** reaches this stage.
* Size: ~**2 mm diameter**.
* Called **Graafian follicle**.
* Oocyte + surrounding cells (**corona radiata**) break away → float in **follicular fluid**.
* Wall ruptures → contents released into **peritoneum** (ovulation).

#### Oocyte Numbers
| Stage | Number |
| :--- | :--- |
| Foetus at 5 months gestation | **7 million** oocytes |
| At birth | **~1 million** oocytes |
| At puberty | **~40,000** oocytes |
| Undergo ovulation during reproductive years | **~400** |

### Corpus Luteum
* Formed **after ovulation**.
* Walls of empty follicle collapse and fold.
* Granulosa cells of theca externa enlarge → **luteal cells** → secrete hormones.
* If pregnancy: corpus luteum **persists**.
* Otherwise: degenerates after **10-12 days**.
* Connective tissue cells enlarge → white → **Corpus Albicans**.
* Eventually **shrinks and disappears**.

### Uterine Tubes (Fallopian Tubes)
* **Two tubes**, one on each side of uterus.
* Each associated with an **ovary**.
* Each about **10 cm long**.
* Terminal part enlarged → **Infundibulum** → opens into **peritoneal cavity**.
* Opening = **Ostium**.
* **Three Parts**:
  1. **Ampulla**: Longest part, nearer to infundibulum.
  2. **Isthmus**: Narrow, nearer to uterus.
  3. **Uterine (Intramural) Part**: Enters the uterus.

### Uterus
* **Hollow, thick-walled muscular organ**.
* **Pear shaped**.
* Size: ~**7.5 cm long, 5 cm wide**.
* Weight: ~**50 g** (non-pregnant); up to **1 kg** during pregnancy.
* **Parts**:
  * **Fundus**: Larger rounded part.
  * **Body**: Middle part.
  * **Cervix**: Narrower part, directed inferiorly.
* Cervix continues as **cervical canal** → opens into **vagina** through **ostium**.
* **Three Layers** of wall:
  1. **Perimetrium** (Serous layer): Outermost.
  2. **Myometrium** (Muscular coat): Major part.
  3. **Endometrium** (Mucus membrane): Innermost, **functional layer**; undergoes **menstrual changes** and **sloughing** during female sex cycle.

### Vagina
* **Female copulatory organ**.
* **Fibromuscular tube**, about **10 cm long**.
* Extends from **uterus to outside**.
* Functions: intercourse, **menstrual flow**, **child birth**.

## 8. External Female Genitalia (Vulva / Pudendum)

### Vestibule
* Region between **two labia majora**.
* Contains **vaginal opening** and **urethral opening**.
* Surrounded by **mons pubis** (anteriorly) and **labia majora/minora** (laterally).

### Mons Pubis
* **Rounded eminence** anteriorly.
* Made of **subcutaneous adipose connective tissue**.
* Covered by **coarse hair** at puberty.

### Labia Majora
* **Two longitudinal folds of skin**.
* Form **outer boundary** for vestibule.

### Labia Minora
* **Two small skin folds** between labia majora.
* Nearer to **vaginal opening**.

### Clitoris
* **Homologous with male penis**.
* **Erectile structure**.
* Found at **anterior margin of vestibule**.
* **Sensitive region** with **sensory receptors**.

### Hymen Vaginae
* **Thin mucus membrane** within vaginal orifice.
* If completely closes vaginal opening → must be removed for menstrual flow.
* May normally get **torn** during physical exercise in young women.
* May be **absent** in some women.
* **No established function**.

### External Urethral Opening
* About **2.5 cm below clitoris**.
* **Anterior to vaginal opening**.
* Remains as a **small cleft**.
