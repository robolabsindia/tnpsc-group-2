## 4. Protected Areas: National Parks vs. Wildlife Sanctuaries
| Feature | National Park | Wildlife Sanctuary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Dedicated to protecting the environment, natural objects, and wildlife. | Animals are well protected from any disturbance. |
| **Human Habitation** | Not permitted in the main area. | Allowed (with restrictions). |
| **Resource Extraction** | Harvesting timber, cultivation, and collection of forest products are restricted. | Allowed with permission. |
| **Conversion** | Cannot be changed into a Wildlife Sanctuary. | Can be upgraded/changed into a National Park. |
| **Total in India** | **166** | **442** |

## 5. Biosphere Reserves (MAB Programme)
* **Man and Biosphere (MAB)**: India has **18** Biosphere Reserves, out of which **11 are recognized by UNESCO**.
* **First Biosphere Reserve in India**: **Nilgiri** (Established in 1986; UNESCO recognized in 2000).
* **First Marine Biosphere Reserve**: **Gulf of Mannar** (1989).
### UNESCO Recognized Biosphere Reserves (11)
1. Nilgiri (TN, Karnataka, Kerala) - 2000
2. Gulf of Mannar (TN) - 2001
3. Sundarbans (WB) - 2001
4. Nanda Devi (Uttarakhand) - 2004
5. Simlipal (Odisha) - 2008
6. Pachmarhi (MP) - 2009
7. Nokrek (Meghalaya) - 2009
8. Achanakmar-Amarkantak (CG, MP) - 2012
9. Great Nicobar (A&N) - 2013
10. Agasthyamalai (Kerala, TN) - 2016

## 6. Protected Areas in Tamil Nadu
### National Parks
* **Mudumalai** (Nilgiri, 1940): First Modern National Park in South India.
* **Guindy** (Chennai, 1976): 8th smallest National Park in India.
* **Gulf of Mannar Marine NP** (Ramanathapuram, 1980).
* **Mukurthi** (Nilgiri, 1982): Created to protect the keystone species, Nilgiri Tahr.
* **Indira Gandhi NP** (Coimbatore, 1989): Formerly Annamalai NP; **Largest National Park in Tamil Nadu**.

### Wildlife Sanctuaries & Tiger Reserves
* **Kalakkadu** (Tirunelveli, 1962): Habitat for Lion-tailed macaque. (Tiger Reserve since 1988).
* **Point Calimere** (Nagapattinam, 1967): Known for Blackbucks/Deers.
* **Annamalai / Indira Gandhi WLS** (Coimbatore, 1974).
* **Srivilliputhur** (Virudhunagar, 1988): Grizzled Giant Squirrel sanctuary.
* **Sathyamangalam** (Erode, 2008): **Largest Wildlife Sanctuary** in TN. Tiger Reserve (2012) with maximum tiger resident area.
* **Meghamalai** (Theni, 2012): Latest/Last Wildlife Sanctuary declared in TN.

### Project Elephant (1992) Reserves in TN
1. Srivilliputhur (2002)
2. Nilgiri (2003)
3. Coimbatore (2003)
4. Annamalai (2003)

### Bird Sanctuaries (Key ones)
* **Vedanthangal** (Kanchipuram, 1936): Oldest bird sanctuary in TN.
* **Kodiakkarai** (Nagapattinam, 1967).
* **Pulicat Lake** (Thiruvallur, 1980).
* **Suchindram Theroor** (Kanyakumari, 1999).
