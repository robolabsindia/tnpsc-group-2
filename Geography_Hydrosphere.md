# Hydrosphere (TNPSC Group II Geography)

## 1. Ocean Relief
```
```
The ocean floor is not flat; it is as irregular as the continental surface.
* **Continental Shelf**: Coastal part with a gentle slope (1°-2° gradient). Extends to ~100 fathoms (180m). Occupies **7.6%** of the ocean area. Broad in submergence coastlines, narrow in emergence. Rich in sunlight, fostering marine life and **chief fishing grounds** (e.g., North Sea, Grand Bank).
* **Continental Slope**: Steep slope (2°-5° gradient) descending from the shelf to the deep ocean bed (200m to 2500m deep). Constitutes **8.5%** of the ocean floor. Cut by submarine canyons.
* **Continental Rise**: Low gradient area at the foot of the slope formed by accumulated debris. Rich in minerals and some oceanic oil deposits.
* **Trenches (Ocean Deeps)**: Considerable depths associated with subduction zones.
  * **Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep)**: West Pacific. Deepest part (~10,800m to 11,033m).
  * **Tonga Trench**: South Pacific (2nd deepest, ~9,000m).
  * **Puerto Rico Trench (Milwaukee Deep)**: Deepest in the Atlantic.
  * **Java Trench**: Indian Ocean.
* **Abyssal Plain (Deep Sea Plain)**: Deepest and most extensive part. Contains submarine ridges, seamounts, and guyots (flat-topped volcanic peaks eroded by wave action).

## 2. Major Oceans & Features
```
```
### Pacific Ocean
```
```
* **Size/Depth**: Largest (1/3 of world area) and deepest ocean.
* **Features**: Mariana Trench (11,033m), Mindanao Trench (10,500m). Contains over 20,000 islands (mostly coral/volcanic in mid-ocean).

### Atlantic Ocean
```
```
* **Size/Shape**: S-shaped, half the size of the Pacific.
* **Features**: **Mid-Atlantic Ridge** (Telegraph Plateau at 55°N, Dolphin Range in North, Challenger Ridge in South). Divided by the **Romanche Deep** near the equator.
* **Sargasso Sea**: A calm sea within the North Atlantic current circuit, full of *Sargassum* seaweeds. Lacks significant islands and trenches compared to the Pacific.

### Indian Ocean
```
```
* **Features**: Completely land-locked in the north.
* **Ridges**: Lakshadweep-Chagos Ridge, **Ninety East Ridge** (90°E longitude), Carlsberg Ridge.
* **Trench**: Sunda Trench (south of Java).

## 3. Ocean Resources
```
```
* **Mineral Oil**: Found on continental shelves. Major Indian producers: Mumbai High, Bassein, Aliabet, Krishna-Godavari basin.
* **Food Resources**: Major fishing grounds are in the temperate zones of the Northern Hemisphere (e.g., NW Pacific, NW Atlantic).
* **Energy Resources**:
  * **Tidal Energy**: Requires >3m tidal range. India's Potential: Gulf of Khambhat (7000 MW), Gulf of Kutch (1000 MW), Sunderbans (100 MW). NPC set up a 900 MW plant in Kutch.
  * **Wave Energy**: Plant installed at Vizhinjam (Kerala).
  * **OTEC (Ocean Thermal Energy)**: Requires 20°C difference between surface (25°-30°C) and deep water (5°C).
  * **Geothermal**: Parvati Valley (Manikaran, HP) and Puga Valley (Ladakh, J&K).

## 4. Temperature & Salinity
```
```
### Temperature
```
```
* **Average**: 17.2°C (Pacific is slightly warmer).
* **Distribution**: Highest in **tropical regions** (23°-27°C), NOT at the equator (due to high humidity/clouds). Decreases towards poles.
* **Vertical**: Thermocline layer (500-1000m) sees a rapid drop in temperature.

### Salinity
```
```
* **Average**: 35 parts per thousand (35‰).
* **Distribution**: Highest in **Tropical Trade Wind belts** (high evaporation, low rain). Lowest in Polar regions (melting ice).
* **Extremes**: Lake Van (Turkey) - 330‰ (Highest); Dead Sea - 240‰; Red Sea - 37-41‰; Baltic Sea - 11‰ (Lowest, freezes rapidly).
* **Vertical**: Halocline zone shows a rapid decrease in salinity.

## 5. The Tides
```
```
* **Spring Tides**: Occur during New and Full Moons (Sun, Moon, Earth in a straight line). Highest high tides and lowest low tides.
* **Neap Tides**: Occur during 1st and 3rd quarters (Sun and Moon at right angles). Lowest high tides and highest low tides.
* **Bay of Fundy (Canada)**: Highest tidal range (15-20m).
* **Tidal Bore**: A vertical wall of water rushing upstream in narrow estuaries (e.g., **Hooghly River** in West Bengal).

## 6. Ocean Currents
```
```
* **Warm Currents**: Flow from low to high latitudes (e.g., Gulf Stream, Kuroshio, Agulhas, Brazil).
* **Cold Currents**: Flow from high to low latitudes (e.g., Labrador, Californian, Peru, Benguela, West Australian).
* **Key Phenomenon**: The meeting of the warm **Gulf Stream** and cold **Labrador Current** near Newfoundland produces thick fog and rich fishing grounds.
* **Indian Ocean**: Currents in the North change direction seasonally due to Monsoons (**Monsoon Drift**).

## 7. Coral Reefs & Atolls
```
```
* **Conditions**: Tropical, clean sediment-free water, shallow depth (needs sunlight), open seas.
* **Types**: Fringing Reef (Lakshadweep), Barrier Reef (Great Barrier Reef, Australia - largest), Atoll (horseshoe-shaped).
* **Coral Bleaching**: Loss of algae turning corals white (death). Major causes: Global warming, **El Niño** (1983, 1987, 1998), pollution.
* **India**: Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar, Gulf of Kutch, Gulf of Mannar.

## 8. Miscellaneous Facts
```
```
* **Deserts**: Ladakh is the **only cold desert in India**. Others: Sahara, Gobi (cold), Atacama, Kalahari, Namib.
* **Terms**: **Isthmus** (narrow land connecting two large masses, e.g., Panama), **Strait** (narrow waterway, e.g., Dover).
* **Environmental Laws**:
  * Air Act (1981, amended 1987)
  * Biological Diversity Act (2002) -> **NBA HQ in Chennai** (est. 2003)
  * Environment Protection Act (1986)
  * Forest Conservation Act (1980)
